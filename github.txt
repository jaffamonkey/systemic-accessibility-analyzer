An end-to-end accessibility testing platform that runs multi-engine scans and clusters the findings to identify systemic, component-level root causes.

Stop triaging the same accessibility bug 100 times. This platform orchestrates multi-tool a11y scans and groups the results by component, generating interactive dashboards and BI-ready reports to help teams fix systemic issues at the source.

A systemic accessibility analyzer and orchestrator. It runs Playwright-based tools (Axe, IBM, HTMLCS, Lighthouse) against authenticated estates, normalizes the findings, and clusters them to highlight shared component defects rather than isolated page errors.
